## Design Context

### Users
A single learner preparing for AWS Solutions Architect Associate in four days. Long study sessions across desktop and mobile; the primary job is to find knowledge gaps, understand architecture tradeoffs, and practice realistic timed exams.

### Brand Personality
Playful, encouraging, focused. Celebrate real progress without adding pressure or pretending to predict an AWS exam result.

### Aesthetic Direction
A comic-book learning companion: warm paper, deep ink outlines, yellow and blue accents, sticker details, a friendly illustrated cloud, chunky tactile controls. Expressive dashboard, calm readable question screen. Light-first, responsive, accessible, reduced-motion aware.

### Design Principles
1. Make the next useful study action obvious.
2. Accuracy and transparent evidence come before gamification.
3. Keep long scenarios and option explanations comfortable to read.
4. Use expressive illustration and typography with disciplined spacing.
5. Show real progress, never invented scores or activity.
